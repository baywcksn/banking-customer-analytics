# 🏦 Banking Customer & Transaction Analytics

An end-to-end **Data Analytics project** that simulates a digital banking analytics environment.

This project focuses on transforming customer and transaction data into actionable business insights to understand **customer behavior, transaction performance, product and channel usage, customer segmentation, and retention**.

The project is designed to follow a professional analytics workflow, from raw data ingestion and transformation to analysis, visualization, and business insights.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to demonstrate how data can be transformed into meaningful business insights through a structured and reproducible analytics workflow.

The analysis will focus on:

* Transaction performance and trends
* Customer behavior and activity
* Product performance
* Transaction channel performance
* Customer retention and cohort analysis
* RFM customer segmentation
* Potential customer churn patterns
* Interactive business intelligence dashboard

---

## ❓ Business Questions

The project aims to answer several key business questions:

1. How does transaction volume and value change over time?
2. What are the characteristics and activity patterns of customers?
3. Which banking products are most frequently used?
4. How does transaction performance differ across channels?
5. How well do customers return after their first transaction?
6. How can customers be segmented based on their transaction behavior?
7. What patterns may indicate potential customer churn?

## 📊 Planned Analysis

### Transaction Analytics

Analyze transaction volume, transaction value, status, and trends over time.

### Customer Analytics

Understand customer characteristics, activity levels, and transaction contribution.

### Product & Channel Analytics

Analyze product usage and compare transaction activity across different channels.

### RFM Segmentation

Segment customers based on:

* **Recency** — how recently a customer made a transaction
* **Frequency** — how frequently a customer transacts
* **Monetary** — how much transaction value a customer generates

### Retention & Cohort Analysis

Measure customer return behavior and analyze retention patterns across customer cohorts.

### Churn Analysis

Identify customer activity patterns that may indicate potential churn.

## 🧠 Project Approach

The project follows several principles:

* **Business First** — start from business problems and questions.
* **Data Quality** — validate data before analysis.
* **Reproducibility** — make the workflow repeatable.
* **Separation of Concerns** — separate ingestion, transformation, analysis, and visualization.
* **Evidence-Based Insights** — derive insights from data and analysis.
* **Professional Documentation** — document important decisions and findings.
